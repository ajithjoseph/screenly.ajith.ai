    How it works
    • Daily average is the total time divided by the number of days you filled in. Empty days are skipped, so they don't pull the average down.
    • Weekly total is the daily average times 7. Per year is the daily average times 365.
    • "Share of waking hours" assumes you're awake 16 hours a day.
    • Your numbers are saved on this device only, so they're still here next time. Nothing is sent anywhere.
